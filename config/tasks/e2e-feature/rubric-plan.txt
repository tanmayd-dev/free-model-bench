Score each dimension from 1 (unusable) to 10 (exceptional). Be exacting: a
plausible-sounding plan that is vague, that misreads the spec, or that invents
API that is not in the brief should not score above 5 on that dimension.

DIMENSIONS

specificity
  1-3  Says "add recurring support" and little more. No file names, no
       signatures, no output formats.
  4-6  Names modules and roughly what goes in each, but signatures and output
       formats are missing or approximate.
  7-8  Exact files, exact Python declarations, literal format strings and
       exit codes spelled out. Someone could start coding immediately.
  9-10 All of the above plus worked date/number examples that can be checked
       against the brief by hand.

correctness
  1-3  Misses the hard requirements (month-end clamping, non-accumulating
       month arithmetic, inclusive window endpoints, exit code 2, atomic
       writes, the "never raises" contract).
  4-6  Catches most of them but at least one material rule is wrong or absent.
  7-8  Covers the required behaviour; minor gaps that would not break tests.
  9-10 Every stated rule is addressed explicitly, with correct worked
       examples, and the plan calls out the trap of accumulating month shifts
       from the previous occurrence rather than from the anchor.

risk_awareness
  1-3  Treats it as greenfield; never mentions the existing approximation in
       ledger/dates.py, the visible suite, or failure modes.
  4-6  Mentions risks generically without saying where they bite.
  7-8  Identifies the specific landmines in this codebase and says how each
       will be handled.
  9-10 Above plus concrete mitigation/verification for each risk, including
       how to avoid regressing the existing 27 visible tests.

feasibility
  1-3  The plan as written would not run, or requires forbidden dependencies
       or out-of-scope work.
  4-6  Doable but the sequencing would leave things broken mid-way.
  7-8  Sensible order, each step independently verifiable.
  9-10 Order is efficient, dependencies between steps are explicit, and there
       is a clear verification step for each increment.

structure
  1-3  Wall of prose or unrelated digression; impossible to follow.
  4-6  Organised loosely, but sections are missing or duplicated.
  7-8  Clear sections covering files, API, algorithms, output formats, order
       and reuse.
  9-10 Complete, internally consistent, scannable; a reader can find any one
       answer in seconds.

LENGTH IS NOT SCORED. A shorter plan that hits every requirement beats a long
one that does not. Do not reward verbosity, formatting flourish, or hedging.
